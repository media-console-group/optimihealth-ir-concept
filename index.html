Doubt: the auditor’s report on the statements for the years ended September 30, 2025 and 2024 (Davidson & Company LLP, dated May 19, 2026) has a going-concern paragraph saying the company “has suffered recurring losses from operations and has not yet achieved profitable operations that raise substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern.” Filed 424B3, Aug 27 The August 27, 2026 prospectus risk factor says: “We have suffered recurring losses from operations and have a net capital deficiency that raise substantial doubt about our ability to continue as a going concern.” Its MD&A says: “These uncertainties cast significant doubt on the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.” Sufficiency: the interim statements furnished August 14, 2026 say management “has concluded that the Company has sufficient cash resources and expected cash flows to continue its operations and meet its obligations as they become due for at least the next twelve months from the date of these financial statements,” Company statement 6-K, Aug 14 as did the statements furnished June 2. 6-K, Jun 2, MD&A The prospectus also estimates that its cash and the funds raised in its offering “will be sufficient to fund operations for the next 12 months,” and adds that “our operating plan has changed, and may continue to change.” Company statement 424B3, Aug 27 A September 10 press release says of the Phase 2 trial: “we can comfortably fund it with current cash on hand.” Company statement 6-K, Sep 10 Cash was C$106,711 at Mar 31, 2026, before the May 2026 offering, and C$13,073,333 at Jun 30, 2026. 01The companyIncorporated 2020 · British Columbia

The company.

The prospectus prints its earlier name two ways: 1251418 B.C. Ltd. in the summary and business sections, and 1251417 B.C. Ltd. in note 1 to the financial statements. 04Access and programmesWhere supply is lawful, and what is planned

Where the products can be supplied, and what is planned.

The filings give no sourced market-size figure. The one patient-population figure in them is the company’s own, from its September 10 release: major depressive disorder is “among the most common mental health conditions worldwide, affecting an estimated 332 million people, according to the World Health Organization.” Company statement 6-K, Sep 10 This section sets out where the prospectus says the products can lawfully be supplied, where they are used in trials, and where the company says it cannot sell.

A

Australia

The company’s products are available there “where psilocybin and MDMA have been rescheduled to allow prescription by an Authorized Prescriber only for TRD and PTSD.” It launched natural psilocybin capsules there and made an initial shipment of 1,000 capsules in September 2025. Company statement 424B3, Aug 27

Supply runs through a five-year agreement signed February 28, 2023 with KMT Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd, with Mind Medicine acting as guarantor. The company agreed not to supply MDMA or psilocybin products to any other party in Australia, other than for clinical trials; either party can end that exclusivity on nine months’ written notice, and “There are no minimum purchase requirements under the Supply Agreement.” Company statement 424B3, Aug 27

Its June 29, 2026 release calls the latest 1,000-capsule export its third commercial shipment of the 5 mg psilocybin capsules, and its October 20 release plans the next MDMA export before the end of 2026. Company statement 6-K, Jun 29 6-K, Oct 20

B

Canada

Products are available through the Special Access Program. “The SAP patient authorization process has been successfully achieved for MDMA products.” The business overview says the psilocybin product is “currently in phase 2b clinical trials in Canada conducted by third parties.” Another passage, on a supply agreement with ATMA CENA Healthcare Solutions, says: “We are awaiting confirmation from ATMA CENA Healthcare Solutions regarding next steps under the agreement and expect to supply psilocybin extract in connection with their planned clinical trial.” Company statement 424B3, Aug 27

C

Trials elsewhere

“In all other countries, access is restricted to approved clinical trials,” including Israel, where the prospectus says the MDMA product is in phase 2 trials conducted by third parties. Company statement 424B3, Aug 27 A June 10, 2026 release reports the first psilocybin export to the United Kingdom for a Phase 2 trial. 6-K, Jun 10

D

United States

“We are currently restricted from selling our products in the United States,” where, the prospectus says, psilocybin and MDMA are considered Schedule I “controlled” or “scheduled” substances. Company statement 424B3, Aug 27

How supply to Australia works, in the company’s own account

  1. Made in Princeton. The capsules are manufactured at the company’s GMP facility under its Health Canada Drug Establishment Licence. Company statement 6-K, Oct 20
  2. Permits. The company reports receiving a TGA import permit and a Health Canada export permit for 1,000 capsules of 5 mg psilocybin in May 2025. Company statement 424B3, Aug 27
  3. Shipped. The company says its product is “shipped to pharmacy partners globally” and supplies clinics and pharmacies in Australia through Optimi Australia. Company statement 6-K, Jun 29 6-K, Oct 20
  4. Prescribed. Authorized Prescribers may prescribe psilocybin for treatment-resistant depression and MDMA for PTSD. Company statement 424B3, Aug 27
